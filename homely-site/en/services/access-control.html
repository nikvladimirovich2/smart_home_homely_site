Access control

Access control: smart locks, intercoms and gates

Keys get lost and copies get handed around. A smart lock, video intercom and automated gate show you who comes in and when, and let you open the door even when you're not home.

Get a free consultation

Smart locks

We install smart locks and cylinders that unlock with a code, card, fingerprint or phone, with a mechanical key kept as a backup. You can give guests, a babysitter or a tradesperson a temporary code valid only on certain days, and every unlock shows up in the app.

Video intercom, gate and garage

A video intercom shows who's ringing on your phone wherever you are and lets you open the gate or front door with one tap. Existing gate and garage door motors can usually be connected with a smart relay, without replacing the motor.

The gate can open automatically as your car approaches home, and the system tells you if the garage door has been left open too long.

Security first

We choose the lock to suit your door and the level of protection you need, and wherever possible set up access so it doesn't depend on the manufacturer's servers. During a power cut the locks run on batteries, and the mechanical key always opens the door.

Frequently asked questions

What if the lock's battery runs out?
The lock warns you about a low battery well in advance, and most models also have an external emergency power contact or a mechanical key, so a flat battery won't leave you locked out.
Can I keep my building's existing intercom?
Often yes. It depends on the building's intercom system — some can be connected so the call reaches your phone, others we'd recommend replacing. We check this during the site visit.
How much does a smart lock cost?
A smart lock with installation costs roughly €150–260, and a video intercom €180–320. The Premium package includes smart locks, a video intercom and gate automation and starts from €2690.
