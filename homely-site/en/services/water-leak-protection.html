Leak & flood protection

Water leak and flood protection

A burst pipe under the sink or a split washing machine hose can ruin floors, furniture and the ceiling of the neighbour below within hours. A leak sensor with an automatic valve shuts off the water within seconds — even when you're not home.

Get a free consultation

How it works

We place small wireless sensors where leaks usually start: under the sink, washing machine and dishwasher, by the water heater and boiler, and in the bathroom. A motorised valve is fitted on the main water supply. When a sensor detects water the valve closes, a siren sounds and you get a notification on your phone.

Holiday homes and rentals

The worst damage happens when nobody is there to notice. In holiday homes and rental flats the system can also shut off the water automatically when the property is empty, and monitor temperature to warn you about the risk of frozen pipes in winter.

Works without the internet

Sensors and the valve talk locally through Home Assistant, so protection works even when the internet is down. The system checks sensor batteries regularly and tells you in good time when one needs replacing.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a plumber to fit the valve?
The motorised valve is usually mounted on the existing main shut-off valve. If the pipework needs changing, we coordinate with a plumber, and we connect and configure the valve ourselves.
What if a sensor raises a false alarm?
The sensors only react to direct contact with water, so false alarms are rare. After checking, you reopen the valve from the app or by hand.
How much does leak protection cost?
A leak sensor with a motorised valve costs roughly €90–160. The Home package includes leak sensors in the kitchen and bathroom and starts from €1290.
