FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer here? Reach out and we'll respond within a business day.

Do I need internet for my automations to work?
No. We build on Home Assistant running locally in your home, so motion-activated lights, alarms and other core automations keep working even if your internet connection drops. Internet is only needed for remote access and notifications.
Will my data be sent to a manufacturer's cloud?
By default, no. We prioritize local-first devices and keep camera footage and automation logic on hardware inside your home. Cloud features are only enabled if you specifically want them.
Can I add more devices later?
Yes. Every system we install is modular — you can start with a Starter package and add cameras, sensors or automations at any point without replacing existing hardware.
How long does installation take?
A Starter package typically takes 1 day. Home and Premium packages usually take 2–4 days depending on property size and whether new cabling is required.
Do you offer support after installation?
Yes, every package includes a support period with remote monitoring and firmware updates. Extended support plans are available afterward.
Can I control the system without a smartphone?
Yes. Wall-mounted panels, physical switches and voice assistants can all be configured alongside or instead of the mobile app.
