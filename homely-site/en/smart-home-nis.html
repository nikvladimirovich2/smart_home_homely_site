Niš and surroundings

Smart home and CCTV installation in Niš

Homely is based in Niš. We design and install smart homes, CCTV and home networks for apartments, family houses, weekend homes and businesses in Niš and the surrounding area — and for larger projects, elsewhere in Serbia too.

Get a free consultation

Local — literally

Because we're in Niš, the site visit is free and easy to arrange, and if you need anything after installation you're not waiting for someone to come from another city.

We're local in the technical sense too: the systems we install run on Home Assistant on your premises, not on foreign servers. Lights, heating and alarms keep working when the internet goes down.

New-build flat or older house

If you're buying a new-build apartment, that's the ideal moment to plan smart lighting, networking and cameras before the finishing works — that's when installation is simplest and cheapest.

In older houses and apartments we choose wireless solutions and use the existing junction boxes to keep wall chasing to a minimum. A lot can be done without any building work at all.

Frequently asked questions

Is the site visit in Niš free?
Yes. We come over, look at the property and talk through what you need, with no obligation. Within 48 hours of the visit you receive a written proposal with a device list and prices.
How long does installation take?
A Starter package is usually installed in one day. Home and Premium packages usually take 2–4 days, depending on the size of the property and whether new cabling is needed.
Do you work with businesses too?
Yes. For shops, offices and warehouses we install CCTV, access control, networking and lighting and heating automation.
